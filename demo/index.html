A chapter always starts at the first step of the chosen demo version.

    Keyboard shortcuts
    Enter Space →
    Next step. During an animation: finish the current step at once.
    ←
    Previous step (state replayed from the start of the chapter).
    ?
    Show or hide this list.
    Esc
    Close the contents or this list.
    Tab
    Move to the switches on the bottom bar; Enter or Space on a switch changes the mode or the demo version.

    Clicking the scene changes nothing — the autopilot drives the demo.
